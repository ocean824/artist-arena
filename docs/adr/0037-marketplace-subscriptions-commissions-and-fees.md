# ADR-0037: Marketplace, subscriptions, commissions, and fees

**Status:** Proposed
**Decision owners:** Commerce and Finance; founder approval where scope, economics, rights, legal exposure, or irreversible production authority is affected
**Blocking scope:** Products, services, milestones, entitlements, refunds, ratings, splits, and pricing

## Context

The preserved master specification requires this decision to be visible. Owner Directive 0001 makes all described capabilities mandatory V1 construction, but it does not approve an unstated technology, provider, legal interpretation, economic parameter, or production activation. The team must compare realistic options without using an open decision as a reason to defer safe implementation, mocks, simulations, contract surfaces, or tests.

## Decision drivers

The proposal must compare product fit, security, privacy, rights, compliance, determinism, reliability, latency, scale, portability, operational burden, cost, lock-in, licensing, migration, rollback, auditability, accessibility, and the ability to support the complete V1 destination.

## Options considered

1. **Option A — To be completed.** Describe implementation, benefits, limits, risks, cost, migration, and failure behavior.
2. **Option B — To be completed.** Describe implementation, benefits, limits, risks, cost, migration, and failure behavior.
3. **Option C — Status quo or hybrid.** State whether retaining or combining existing approaches is valid.

## Decision

No implementation choice is approved by this scaffold unless the status is `Directed`, and a Directed status approves only the explicit founder requirement described by the title and register. Complete the comparison, name the accountable approver, record the decision date, and link approval evidence before treating a technology or provider choice as accepted.

## Build-versus-activation rule

While this ADR is open, teams must implement interface-neutral contracts, deterministic mocks, simulations, fixtures, tests, and other dependency-valid work. They must not expose real money, production signing, sensitive identity, unapproved provider commitments, public-chain authority, or jurisdiction-restricted behavior without separate activation approval.

## Consequences and follow-up

Record positive and negative consequences, security and privacy impact, economic and rights impact, required migrations, operational ownership, test/evidence requirements, rollout, rollback, and criteria for revisiting the decision. Link affected requirements and backlog tickets.

## Approval evidence

Pending unless a founder directive or signed decision is linked here. Repository text generated during bootstrap is not approval evidence.
